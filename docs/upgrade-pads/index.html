Proposal · unlock pads in the garage and the Spray Bay Built · branch upgrade-pads

Every pad says what it buys, before you pay

Inside the garage and the Spray Bay, every pad shows only a price: $1.5K, $4K. You find out what you bought after the money is gone. The fix reuses the tag and the bottom dock that the town's building pads already have, and adds two things: a faded preview of the thing, standing where it will go, and a before → after line ("Colours 3 → 4"). Paying works exactly as it does now. All the screenshots below come from the live prototype running in the real game.

1What goes wrong today

  • Only a price, 24 times. All 13 garage pads and 11 Spray Bay pads show a cash icon and a number. No word, no picture.
  • The spot doesn't tell. Tank and lift pads stand where the thing goes. Hires, the coffee cart, the billboard, the lamps and the stripe kit sit by a wall or a door.
  • No "what changes". The toast says "Painter hired!" after you paid. Nothing says how much faster, how many more, or what you no longer have to do.
  • "MAX" reads as maxed out. Full tanks say MAX in coral next to the pads. It means "full of paint", but beside upgrades it reads as "no more upgrades".

3The five pieces

Picture tag

Lies on the pad, like the town's building tags. The icon is the thing you buy: the tank in its own colour, the worker in their uniform, the lift, the lamp. It replaces the floating "$1.5K" pill.

Can I pay?

When your cash covers what is left, the icon hops every few seconds. When it doesn't, the price is dimmed. No new colours: mustard is still "buy", teal with an arrow is still "upgrade".

Preview

Within 3.5 m a faded copy (30%) stands where the thing will go; on the pad it gets stronger (55%). Gone as you walk off. No shadow, never blocks.

Before → after

One or two chips in the dock: a count (Lifts 2 → 3), a time (Curing 6 s → 3 s), a multiplier (×1.6) or who does a job (You → Valet).

FULL, not MAX

A full tank or shelf says FULL. Same coral pill, same meaning ("come and take it"), no longer confused with a maxed upgrade.

6Rules

  1. Nothing floats over the pad. The tag lies on the pad and grows sideways. The details are in the dock at the bottom of the screen.
  2. Paying is unchanged. Stand on the pad, the cash drains in, part payments stay on the pad. The dock takes no touches.
  3. The preview only shows where the thing will really stand. Tanks, shelves, booths, lifts, the coffee cart, the billboard, and staff at their post. Things with no single spot (IR lamps, the stripe kit) get the dock only.
  4. On foot only. Tags hide and previews fade while you drive, as the building pads do.
  5. The words live in one table per workshop, next to the prices. The numbers in "before → after" are worked out from the config, never typed in.

7Your call

  1. The preview. It's a hint, not a real object: a faded copy that only shows within 3.5 m. If it feels off to you, the tag and the dock work without it. I'd keep it: it is the one piece that shows where a hire or a tank goes.
  2. Hop on every pad I can pay, or only one? Two pads are on screen at most. If both hop, the guide's arrow still says which one is next. I'd hop both.
  3. FULL instead of MAX on full tanks and shelves. Small, but it removes the "maxed" reading.

8What it touches

  • src/game/pads.js: createChainPads gives each pad a ground tag, dock info and a preview (PayPad already supports ground and dock)
  • src/game/unlock-info.js (new): name, icon, what, before → after per unlock type, for the garage, the Spray Bay and the Club
  • src/game/facility.js, paint.js (and the Club when it is built): a previewModel(fac, u) hook that builds the faded copy and says where it stands
  • src/ui/icons.js: 12 new icons (painter, mechanic, valet, master tech, lift, booth, IR lamp, stripes, coffee, billboard, bar, camera)
  • src/ui/paydock.js + ui.css: the before → after row; the tag's hop and dimmed price
  • src/game/stations.js: FULL instead of MAX
  • Unchanged: prices, order, pad positions, the pay rules, the guide pointer, the building pads in town

Decided: keep the preview, hop on every pad you can pay, FULL instead of MAX. The screenshots came from a throwaway prototype that patched the running game. The words on this page are read from the game's own src/game/unlock-info.js.
