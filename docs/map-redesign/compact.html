B keeps what A argued for: a real reason to use your own car, the late-game places getting room, and a carport for the pickup. It leaves out what makes A an open world: districts with no cars in them, new job types, long drives, and the systems those long drives would need.

2Everything that changes

Stays

All of Harbor Street and Main Street: the garage lot (LAYOUT untouched), tow shed, lounge, car wash, Spray Bay, Pump & Go, Quick-Fit, the ferry quay, Parts Co., the Greasy Spoon and the Academy. The traffic ring, the tunnels, the bus, today's 11 rescue spots and the 16 golden wrenches (three of them move up to Crown Street).

Moves up to Crown Street

Rust & Gold Salvage goes from a 19 m plot to a real yard with room for its stands, crane and crusher. Platinum Club leaves the home block, with room for its red-carpet drop-off. Bay Classics goes next door to the club. Their floor plans don't change; they get a new origin.

New

Crown Street, two short avenue extensions (West Ave and East Ave carry on past Main Street), and an alley between the diner and the Academy straight up to the club's door (the Academy shifts 1 m). Your pickup and its carport on the club's old plot, next to the garage. Two free plots on Main Street are left empty for later.

3Three streets, south to north

Each row up is a little later in the game. Click a card to fly the map there, or a place to open it.

4Your car in B: a tool, not the game

  • Same pickup as in A. It rolls out of the carport when the town opens (valet + a mechanic hired). You get in at its door ring and out in any P bay. It drives with the joystick like the tow truck does today.
  • Walking still works everywhere. The furthest place is … on foot. The pickup halves the Crown Street trips (garage → Platinum Club: … by car, … on foot), and its bed carries lunch boxes to your crews.
  • Nothing extra to learn. No route line, no autopilot, no calling your car: every trip is short enough to just drive. Pro Gear sells a couple of items for it (engine, bed size). That's all.

5Jobs: only the ones the game already has

Rescue calls

As today, on Harbor Street and Main Street, plus two new spots on Crown Street. The tow truck and its pay stay the same.

The project car

Buy a wreck on Crown Street and tow it down West Ave to the garage. Fix it on a lift, paint it at the Spray Bay, then drive it back up Crown Street to the club's turntable and next door to Bay Classics. Each leg is a 5–12 s drive.

Lunch run, tills, wrenches

The Greasy Spoon's lunch boxes ride in the pickup's bed. Tills at the diner, Pump & Go and Quick-Fit are collected as today. The 16 golden wrenches stay; three of them move up to Crown Street.
