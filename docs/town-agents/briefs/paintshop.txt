YOUR ROLE: `paintshop` — the Spray Bay paint shop (TOWN.md section 4.8; config PAINT, PAINT_COLORS, PAINT_UNLOCKS). YOUR FILE: src/game/paint.js (new; more files of your own in src/game/ if you need them).

Build it as a facility on the framework described in TOWN.md section 6a: own customers and lane, spray booths as bays with the mask -> spray -> cure -> done sequence, paint tanks as stations dispensing colour canisters, waiting customers, painters and the prep tech as staff, the 11-step unlock chain with its pads, payout via resprayPayout, shared streak, offline contribution.

What makes this place its own game and must come through in play: reading which colour a customer wants and fetching exactly that; the colour visibly sweeping over the car while you spray (car.setColor with progress, mist fx, spray sfx, the character's spraying pose); and the cure timer that frees you to start another car, so two booths become a juggling act. Customers never ask for the colour their car already has, nor for a colour you do not stock. Stripe jobs (after the stripe kit) need the body colour first, then one canister of the stripe colour. Wrong canisters can be scrapped at a bin inside the hall.
