   - Real scale: concrete slabs about 1.5-3 m with thin saw-cut joints, not 4 m tiles with giant drawn cracks.   - Move the 'garden' parking bay, golden wrench w06 and the wash pay pad and for-sale sign if the new footprint covers them; keep them reachable.
- Route of a car that takes a wash:
  1. It leaves its lift as today, along the exit lane (z 3.0) to the pickup stop, where the owner gets in.
  2. Then it carries on east past the exit driveway (x 13.5) into the wash entry.
  3. It stops briefly at the pay kiosk on the driver's side.
  4. It rolls slowly through soap arch, brushes, rinse, blower.
  5. It leaves at the east end into the east alley (x 24.2..27.2) and turns south to Harbor St (z 11 -> lane 12.8), where it drives east and out like every other leaving car.
  Cars that do not take a wash leave straight down the exit driveway as today. The exit driveway becomes a plain driveway: no arches.
- Who takes a wash:
  - Level 1: only dirty cars (WASH.dirtyChance of arrivals are dirty, `setDirt`).
  - From level 2 (wax & polish): every leaving car.
  - Put the rule in the config, for example `WASH.cleanCarsFromLevel: 2`.
- Camera and realism:
  - The car must stay visible the whole way through. A real tunnel has a roof and walls, so use an open steel gantry frame with a see-through polycarbonate roof, and keep the south side open or glazed (low wall at most).
  - Order along the drive direction is physically right: soap, then brushes, then rinse, then blower.
  - Foam only builds up after the soap arch. Brushes spin and lean in only while a car is at them; nozzles spray only while a car is under them.
  - The car is wet after the rinse, dry after the blower, then clean. Wax tier: coloured foam and a gloss at the end; turbo tier: bigger blower and neon.
  - A car queues behind another if the tunnel is busy; it never drives through it.
  - The building must read as a car wash at a glance: entry sign with an arrow, the kiosk, guide rails on the floor, a drain grate, the exit sign, vacuum stations in the park strip if room allows.
