YOUR ROLE: `painthall` — build the Spray Bay paint shop BUILDING: the hall, the spray booth and the paint tanks.
Design by the lead (2026-11-13). Read this whole file before writing code.

WHY THIS EXISTS
The player bought the Spray Bay and got an empty plot: buying clears the vacant-lot dressing (env-plots.js), and the
"building" that replaces it is createPaintHall(), still a stub that returns an empty group. createSprayBooth and
createPaintTank are plain grey boxes. You replace all three with the real thing. The paint shop GAMEPLAY
(src/game/paint.js, brief paintshop.txt) does not exist yet and is not your job, so the hall must look complete
and believable on its own (bought, no booths or tanks yet), and also with booths and tanks standing in it.

THE CAMERA (decides the design; check everything from it)
- Game camera: offset (0, 29, 17.3) from the player, fov 44, portrait phone (~390 x 844). It looks north and
  down at ~59 degrees. On a phone you see ~14 m across, so never the whole 21.4 m hall at once: every part of the
  hall must read on its own (west end, middle, east end).
- Rule of thumb: an object of height h hides the ground about 0.6 x h NORTH of it. Tall things at the back wall
  hide only the back lane; anything tall in the forecourt or over a booth hides what is just north of it.
- Visible faces: tops, south (+z) faces, and the side faces that point towards the screen centre. North faces
  are never seen. The hall's EAST OUTER wall is seen from the alley / tow shed (that is the view in the player's
  screenshot); the west outer wall from West Avenue.
- No roofs over anything gameplay uses (house rule: enterable buildings have 2.6 m walls, no roof, open south).

and tow shed (steel + mustard). The Spray Bay's own identity: a bright, clean paint shop.
