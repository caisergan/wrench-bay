Proposal · building pads

Building pads that never cover the building

Today every property pad carries a big card that floats above the pad. The camera looks down from the south, so "above the pad" on screen is the building behind it: the card sits on the door, the parking bay and the signs. The fix: the price is printed on the pad, the name appears only when you walk up, and the details open at the bottom of the screen while you stand on the pad.

1What goes wrong today

  • Wrong side. The card's tail points at the pad and the body grows up the screen, which is always the building.
  • Always full size. About 130 × 120 px, over a quarter of the screen width, from any distance, even when you only walk past.
  • Upgrades say little. Same card plus a small "Upgrade Lv 2" ribbon: no current level, no levels left, no "what changes".
  • Small bug. The fame line ("Fame 6") wraps and runs out of the card's right edge on every card that needs fame.

2The design: show more the closer you get

Same tow shed, same pad, three distances. Nothing ever sits above the pad on screen.

3The same rules on every building

4Every state

Tag on the pad

Dock while you stand on the pad

5Rules

  1. Nothing above the pad. The tag is centred on the pad and only grows sideways. No pad UI may reach up the screen.
  2. Detail follows intent. Far: icon + price. Within 3.5 m: + name and level. On the pad: the dock with everything.
  3. The dock never takes touches. Paying stays "stand on the pad". Dragging anywhere still walks you off.
  4. Tags hide while you drive. Pads only work on foot, and a tag must never float over a car driving across the pad.
  5. Colour = kind. Mustard: buy. Teal with an arrow: upgrade. Grey with a padlock: locked. Green: bought.
  6. Pads stay floor paint. They stay where they are, at each lot's front edge; the For Sale and Coming Soon signs stay the in-world cue.
  7. The guide pointers make room. While the dock is up, the bottom edge pointers move above it.

6What it touches

  • src/ui/labels.js: new tag label (icon, price, near state, kinds), replaces the sale card
  • src/ui/paydock.js (new): the bottom dock: show, progress, bought, hide
  • src/ui/ui.css: tag + dock styles; the old card styles go
  • src/game/pads.js: PayPad reports near / on / paying to its label and the dock
  • src/game/town.js: property and Main Street gate pads use the tag; hide while driving
  • src/config.town.js: an icon per property, a short title + perk per level (diner, gas and Quick-Fit levels 2 to 5 have no text yet)
  • src/ui/guidance.js: lift the bottom pointers while the dock is up
